About InAmigos Foundation

Founded on September 23, 2020, by Mr. Govind Shukla (Founder & CEO), InAmigos Foundation is a Section 8 registered non-profit organization licensed by the Central Government, headquartered in Chhattisgarh, India.

We believe in the power of collective action—every contribution is directed towards essential causes such as food distribution, education, women empowerment, animal welfare, environmental sustainability, and social inclusion programs across India.

With a strong network of dedicated professionals, volunteers, and corporate partners, we work tirelessly to bridge gaps in education, healthcare, and economic opportunities for underprivileged communities.

Section 8 Registered 80G & 12A Certified CSR-1 Registered ISO 9001:2015 NGO Darpan - NITI Aayog

Our Mission, Vision & Values

Guided by the principles of inclusivity, empathy, and collaboration, addressing the diverse needs of the communities we serve.

🎯

Our Mission

To empower underprivileged communities by providing access to education, healthcare, and economic opportunities while fostering sustainable development and social inclusion across India.

👁️

Our Vision

To create a more equitable, inclusive, and compassionate society where every individual has the opportunity to thrive, learn, and contribute to the collective well-being of their communities.

💎

Our Values

Humanity is our only religion. We uphold transparency, accountability, and integrity in all our operations while promoting arts, commerce, sports, and environmental protection.

Our Key Initiatives

Six transformative projects addressing critical social needs across education, empowerment, environment, and community welfare.

🍲 Food & Clothing Distribution

Project SEVA

Providing essential food, clothing, and basic necessities to underprivileged communities. Our volunteers spread warmth and hope by reaching those in need, bringing comfort, confidence, and smiles.

Food Distribution Clothing Drive Community Support
📚 Education for All

Project Bachpanshala

Ensuring quality education for underprivileged children by nurturing young minds, encouraging creativity, building confidence, and helping them grow with the right values and opportunities for a brighter tomorrow.

Education Child Development Learning Support
👩‍💼 Women Empowerment

Project UDAAN

Empowering women through comprehensive skill development programs and pathways to financial independence. Building confidence, capabilities, and sustainable livelihoods for women across communities.

Skill Development Financial Independence Leadership
🌳 Environmental Conservation

Project PRAKRITI

Dedicated to environmental conservation and sustainability efforts including tree plantation drives, awareness campaigns on water conservation, and promoting eco-friendly practices in communities.

Tree Plantation Water Conservation Sustainability
🐾 Animal Welfare

Project JEEV

Comprehensive animal welfare initiatives including rescue, protection, and feeding programs. Caring for voiceless creatures, especially providing water and food for birds and street animals during harsh seasons.

Animal Rescue Feeding Programs Bird Care
💼 Skill Development

Project VIKAS

Enhancing employability through comprehensive skill development programs. Training youth and adults across communities with practical skills, certifications, and career guidance for sustainable livelihoods.

Employability Training Career Development

Creating Lasting Social Impact

Our holistic approach addresses diverse community needs through targeted initiatives and grassroots involvement.

📖

Education & Child Development

Through Project Bachpanshala and Anganwadi support, we ensure children receive quality education, creative learning opportunities, and the values needed to become confident future leaders.

💪

Women Empowerment

Project UDAAN provides skill development, financial literacy, and economic independence pathways, enabling women to break barriers and achieve self-sufficiency in their communities.

🌍

Environmental Sustainability

Project PRAKRITI leads conservation efforts including World Water Day celebrations, tree plantation drives, and awareness campaigns promoting sustainable practices for future generations.

🤝

Community Development

Project SEVA addresses immediate needs through food distribution, clothing drives, and inclusive outreach to marginalized communities including the Kinnar (third-gender) community with dignity and respect.

🎓

Employability & Skills

Project VIKAS enhances youth employability through practical training, internship opportunities, digital skills, and career guidance—creating pathways to sustainable livelihoods.

🐕

Animal Welfare

Project JEEV ensures compassionate care for animals through rescue operations, feeding programs for birds and street animals, and awareness about humane treatment of all living beings.
